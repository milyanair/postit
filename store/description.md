# Post It

**Summary (one line):** Floating sticky notes on every page: pin, color, archive and share them

**Description:**

**Your sticky notes, right where you work: on every Nextcloud page.**

Post It adds a tiny sticky-note launcher to the corner of every page. Click it and a note pops up. Jot something down, pin it, and it keeps floating over Files, Talk, Calendar and every other app while you work.

### Highlights
- 📝 **Rich notes:** bold, lists, headings, links, emoji and pasted images
- 📌 **Pin it:** pinned notes follow you across apps; the rest tidy themselves away
- 🎨 **Colors and sizes:** drag, resize and recolor each note; it reopens exactly where you left it
- 🗂 **All notes in one place:** right-click the launcher to find any note, with quick delete and archive
- 📦 **Archive and export:** a side-by-side panel of active and archived notes, with CSV export
- 👥 **Share with your team:** everyone edits the same note, while position, size and pinning stay personal
- ↗️ **Open all, close all:** bring every note back at once, or clear your screen in one click

### Privacy
Notes are stored in your own Nextcloud database, per user. Content is sanitized on the server, and images are kept in the app's own storage and are only shown to people the note is shared with. Nothing leaves your server.

### For admins
- Limit Post It to selected groups
- Sharing follows your server's sharing rules (user enumeration and group restrictions)
- When an account is deleted, its notes, shares and settings are cleaned up

### Requirements
- Nextcloud 32 to 35
- No other apps needed
